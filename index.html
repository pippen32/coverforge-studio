🤝 Straight Talk: How Our Covers Are Made

Many CoverForge covers combine AI-assisted artwork with professional manual finishing — typography, composition, color grading and print preparation are done by hand, by a designer, for your genre. We tell you this up front because Amazon KDP requires authors to disclose AI-generated content when publishing, and we flag clearly — before delivery — whether your cover includes AI-generated elements so you can declare it correctly in KDP.

Prefer zero generative AI? Choose the “stock + design” option in your brief: your cover is built from licensed stock photography and hand design only. Same price, same revision process — your call.

Questions Authors Ask

What do you need from me to start?

A short brief: your title, author name (or pen name), genre, a one-paragraph summary, 2–3 comparable covers you like, and — for print — your trim size and page count once your manuscript is formatted. If the page count isn't final yet, we design the front first and finish the spine when it is.

How many revisions do I get?

Revision rounds continue until you approve a final cover. We work in clear rounds — you comment, we adjust typography, imagery or layout — and final print-ready files are only produced after your approval.

What files do I receive?

Ebook plan: KDP-ready JPG (2560 × 1600). Ebook + Print: the ebook file plus a print-ready PDF of the full wrap (front, spine, back) at your trim size, and 3 promo graphics. Premium: everything plus the full promo kit, a 3D mockup and a series style guide.

Do I own the cover?

Yes — on final delivery you receive the finished cover files for use on your book and its promotion. Source/artwork components may include licensed stock or AI-assisted elements as disclosed in your delivery note; tell us in the brief if you need the no-generative-AI “stock + design” route.

What about Amazon KDP's AI disclosure?

KDP asks authors to disclose AI-generated content, including cover art, when publishing. Your delivery note states plainly whether AI-generated elements were used, so you can answer KDP's question accurately in under a minute. If you chose the “stock + design” option, your note will say so.

Can you match my existing series look?

Yes — send your previous covers in the brief and we'll design to your series' fonts, palette and layout language. The Premium plan includes a written style guide so future books stay consistent.

How long does it take?

Concepts typically arrive within a few business days of a complete brief; the full timeline depends on revision rounds and, for print, on your final page count. Premium orders take a priority slot.
